**Name**\
LINTUL6

**Program type**\
FORTRAN

**Available since**\
Mon, 12/12/2011 - 12:00

**Description**\
LINTUL-6 model  is a simple crop growth model to simulate crop growth of many different crops under potential, water limited, and N limited crop growth conditions. The model can simulate crop rotations, however, the model does not simulate long-term organic matter decomposition and N cycling in the soil.
